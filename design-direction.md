# The Wendy Collective — Experience Direction

## Brand Idea

**Collect experiences, not just reservations.** The Wendy Collective will feel like a private conversation with a deeply knowledgeable travel advisor: cinematic enough to stir anticipation, but always grounded in clarity, care, and a simple next step.

## Information Architecture

| Route | Purpose | Primary conversion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Establish the brand’s point of view; introduce services, featured travel collections, planning approach, and consultation invitation. | Plan Your Journey |
| `/about` | Build personal trust through Wendy’s philosophy, expertise, and individualized working style. | Start a Conversation |
| `/destinations` | Present Caribbean, Mexico, all-inclusive escapes, group travel, and cruises as curated planning collections. | Design This Escape |
| `/faq` | Remove booking friction by answering common planning, fee, support, and group questions. | Ask a Question / Start an Inquiry |
| `/contact` | Capture a detailed, high-intent trip brief. | Send My Trip Brief |
| `/private-experience` | Welcome returning clients to a protected space after sign-in. | Enter Private Experience |

## Homepage Narrative

The homepage moves from cinematic possibility to a specific invitation to plan. It begins with the travel-film hero and two equal calls to action: **Plan Your Journey** for high-intent clients and **Explore Destinations** for early-stage inspiration. The page then frames what Wendy plans, prompts people to explore a preferred travel style, preserves the existing **Why Choose Wendy** promise, outlines the planning process, reserves a genuine-client-story module for approved material only, and closes with a concise trip-brief invitation.

## Visual System

| Element | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Color | Inky black-blue, ivory, aged brass, smoked sand, and saturated ocean accents. |
| Typography | Editorial serif for emotional headlines paired with a precise modern sans-serif for interface and detail. |
| Image treatment | Full-bleed, filmic, warm, natural, and free of visible logos or artificial gloss. |
| Motion | Gentle image parallax, masked reveals, hover translations, and page fades. All nonessential motion is disabled when a visitor requests reduced motion. |
| Interaction | Clear, high-contrast calls to action; concise labels; visible keyboard focus; mobile-first menu and trip brief. |

## Inquiry Flow

Visitors can begin from any consultation CTA. The trip brief gathers name, contact, travel type, destinations of interest, date flexibility, budget guidance, group size, and the priorities that will make the journey meaningful. Submission saves the brief, confirms receipt in the interface, and sends Wendy an owner notification with the submitted details.

## Content Governance

The initial site uses no invented testimonials, ratings, personal credentials, partner relationships, or service fees. The “Client Stories” section is designed as an editorial placeholder and should only be activated with Wendy’s written approval of genuine client feedback. Wendy’s personal biography, credentials, exact fees, direct phone number, privacy notice, and destination/partner claims remain explicitly editable launch content.
