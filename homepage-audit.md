# Public Experience Audit

## Confirmed Strengths

The site has a consistent quiet luxury travel identity across the desktop and mobile experience. Its deep marine tones, warm ivory space, brass accents, editorial type, cinematic hero, and authentic Wendy photography create a personal travel advisor experience rather than a generic booking engine.

## Refinements to Implement

The Wendy Way feature should become a more central opening editorial composition. The enlarged portrait is stronger than before, but it should be brighter and better aligned with the opening copy so the unused left space becomes a deliberate personal introduction rather than a separate margin.

The homepage closing call to action needs a clearer invitation. The phrase beginning “Tell Wendy what you have been…” does not complete the intended thought clearly, so it should be rewritten around sharing a travel idea or dream.

The About page needs an anchored editorial element in its open left area below the main image to balance the long-form story. The Destinations collection should continue using varied visual content and retain one clear Wendy in the World feature. The trip brief sidebar requires a narrower text measure and more considered line breaks so short words do not visually orphan on their own line.

Private Experience should no longer appear in the public header or public footer navigation. The protected route can remain available for Wendy, with a discreet footer login intended for her own access.

## Validation Scope

Desktop review covered the homepage, About, Destinations, a destination guide, FAQ, Contact, and Private Experience. Mobile review covered the homepage, About, Destinations, FAQ, and Contact. The planned changes focus on public clarity, conversion flow, and visual balance without altering the secure inquiry or protected request mechanisms.
