# Grimsley Final Proposal Flow Audit

## Confirmed strengths

The proposal now establishes the sailing and group context immediately, uses approved Mardi Gras imagery, offers a direct official Carnival ship link, explains cabins with an advisor-led estimate, and keeps the request safely separate from booking. The mobile page retains a visible return-to-top action.

## Final refinement decisions

The new **Experience Mardi Gras** card collection is the most complete ship-overview section. The older dark three-item ship summary repeats those same benefits and should be removed so families encounter the experience story once, near the opening.

The proposed final sequence is: opening and sailing facts; Experience Mardi Gras; ship-at-a-glance facts when available; itinerary; inclusions and optional items; cabin starting points; cabin and extras planner; Vacation Protection; travel documents; frequently asked questions; the advisor-led process; cabin request.

The cabin price is the primary decision point and should remain before the interactive planner. Document and FAQ sections remain before the request so families can self-serve common questions without leaving the proposal. The final request section continues to state that it does not hold a cabin or create a reservation.
