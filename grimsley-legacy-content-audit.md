## Required intake elements

The shareable proposal needs selected room and cabin preference, household traveler count, legal name, title, date of birth, residence, email, phone, optional Carnival VIFP number, military or interline or senior qualifiers, cabin-area preference, requested amenities, family notes, consent to contact, and acknowledgement that the request does not create a booking. It must exclude passport number, passport image, payment-card number, supplier sign-in credentials, and any other high-risk data.
