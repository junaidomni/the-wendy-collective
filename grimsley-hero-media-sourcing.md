# Grimsley Hero Media Sourcing Notes

## Reviewed sources

Carnival’s official Mardi Gras page confirms the relevant ship areas: Mardi Gras, onboard activities, dining, staterooms, and destinations. Carnival Cruise Line’s official YouTube playlist for Mardi Gras includes published clips for BOLT, Ultimate Playground, Summer Landing and Lido, and Welcome Home Mardi Gras.

## Delivery decision

Third party walkthroughs are excluded. The initial direct YouTube embed was also rejected for the private proposal hero because the embedded player displayed an automated sign-in confirmation instead of reliable visual media in the page environment. The hero must use a stable, direct media asset or a client-side still sequence with a static fallback, not an embedded player that can surface controls, prompts, or third party interface elements.
