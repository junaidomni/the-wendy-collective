## Sequence Decision

The revised film removes all recurring sailboat transitions and the weak final sailboat scene. The retained Disney family, Greece, Alaska, and Africa chapters are separated by one selected real-world adult-couple travel moment and gentle dissolves. This gives the hero an intentional destination rhythm rather than a repeated transition device.

The site will not copy YouTube footage, travel-blog video, or cruise-line marketing content without specific written permission or a license. This avoids using material that is merely publicly viewable but not licensed for reuse.
