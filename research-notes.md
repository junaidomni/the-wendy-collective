# The Wendy Collective — Research Notes

## Current Brand Foundations

The existing site positions **The Wendy Collective** as a personal travel-advisory service focused on thoughtful, customized planning. Its existing offer areas include group cruises, all-inclusive resorts, destination weddings, honeymoons, family vacations, and custom itineraries. The retained brand voice is warm, organized, and reassuring: an advisor who listens, recommends thoughtfully, makes tradeoffs clear, and remains available when plans shift. Source: [thewendycollective.com](https://thewendycollective.com).

## Direction to Carry Forward

The experience should feel like a refined travel journal rather than an online booking engine. It will use editorial typography, a restrained warm-neutral palette with deep ocean and sunlit accents, layered imagery, quiet motion, and generous whitespace. The information architecture will direct prospective clients from inspiration to a simple, high-intent trip inquiry. The existing **Why Choose Wendy** area will remain, while client stories will be structured as an optional, clearly labeled content module that is only populated with approved genuine client feedback.

## Content Still Needed From Wendy

Before launch, collect Wendy’s approved biography, certifications or host-agency affiliations, headshot, preferred contact details, service-fee wording, desired destinations and partners, genuine approved client feedback (if used), privacy-policy terms, and the exact contact address to receive trip-inquiry notifications.
