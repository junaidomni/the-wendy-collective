# Google Business Profile Recommendation

## Recommendation

The Wendy Collective should create a Google Business Profile **after Wendy confirms the final public business name, service area, business phone number, and final website domain**. It is free and lets a business manage how it appears on Google Search and Maps. Google explicitly supports service businesses that operate without a physical storefront and allows them to define service areas and specialties. [1]

For a travel advisor, the profile provides a local discovery and trust layer beside the website. It can present the business name, travel advisor category, service area, business hours, website link, approved Wendy photos, and eventually real client reviews. It should not be created with a placeholder name, an inaccurate address, or invented service areas. Google requires business information to be accurate and permits only one profile per business. [2]

## Setup Requirements

| Item | Recommended approach |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | Use “The Wendy Collective” exactly as it appears on the website and other business materials. |
| Business category | Select the narrowest accurate primary category available for Wendy’s actual business activity. |
| Address or service area | Use a precise service area if Wendy does not meet clients at a public storefront. Do not invent a public address. |
| Contact details | Use the final business phone number, business email, and final website domain. |
| Photos | Use the approved Wendy portraits and original business or travel imagery already present on the site. |
| Verification | Google determines the available verification method, which may include video, phone, text, email, live video call, or mail. [3] |

After verification, Google recommends maintaining accurate, complete business information. New profiles can take time to appear in search results, and updates may take several days to display. [4]
